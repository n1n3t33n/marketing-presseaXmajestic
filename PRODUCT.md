# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page, no build step. Media files live beside the HTML in `assets/` so the kiosk works offline.

## Users

Children aged 6 to 12 and their families, cinema-goers at Majestic Ivoire (Abidjan) for the release of the film « À la croisée des mondes ». They use a tablet in the cinema hall, standing, often with a parent or a hostess nearby. Some young children read slowly or not at all.

Secondary user: the hostess / operations team, who read the result code, hand out the matching Mini Présséa flavour, and consult the back-office (long press on the brand, PIN).

## Product Purpose

« Trouve ton pouvoir »: a short quiz (1 favourite-flavour question + 4 Yes/No questions), a 3-5 s suspense animation, then the reveal of one of 6 powers, each tied to a Mini Présséa flavour. The child shows a code to the hostess and receives that flavour to taste.

Success (brief targets, to be validated): ≥ 500 powers discovered, completion rate > 85 %, full path under 2 minutes, no power under 10 % of results.

## Positioning

The child's favourite flavour influences the power they get (about 2 out of 3 times); otherwise the result invites them to try a new flavour. Each power is embodied by an existing Mini Présséa fruit-superhero mascot.

## Operating Context

Tablets in the cinema hall, kiosk mode, offline-first. Auto-return to home after 60 s of inactivity. Part of a wider activation (mysterious letter, mascot, kits) under the AGROCI × Majestic Ivoire partnership.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 6 flavours → 6 powers: Orange/Énergie, Ananas/Éclat, Pomme/Sagesse, Cocktail/Métamorphose, Goyave/Intuition, Mangue/Bravoure.
- Attribution rule from the brief (points per answer, top-4 compatible powers, favourite flavour wins if compatible) — must not change.
- Back-office: participations, distribution by power and by favourite flavour, completion rate, CSV export.
- Audio reading of questions (requested by the brief): browser speech synthesis, fr-FR.
- No personal data collected.
- Open: QR code on the result card, sync of results when network returns, final power names and question wording, film visual usage (must be validated with Majestic Ivoire).

## Brand Commitments

Mini Présséa charter, taken from the supplied media (`camion presssea_230X42cm.jpg`, `Mini Présséa *.mp4`): lime-to-green radial gradient ground, green script « Présséa » logo with red « Mini », red hand-lettered tagline « Petits mais fruités ! » outlined in white, fruit superhero mascots wearing masks, « Source d'énergie » pill. Confirmed direction with the user: Présséa charter + touches of magic for the film universe. French copy, short sentences.

## Product Principles

1. A child who cannot read must still be able to finish: big targets, pictures, audio.
2. No wrong answer: every choice feels like progress and celebration.
3. The flavour is the hero: every power is shown through its mascot and pack colour.
4. Under two minutes, kiosk-proof: nothing can trap or exit the child.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Young children (6+), mixed reading levels: audio option, large touch targets (≥ 64 px), reduced-motion respected, colour never the only carrier of meaning (labels on every flavour).
